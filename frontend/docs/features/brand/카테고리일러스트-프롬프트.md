# 카테고리 일러스트 생성 프롬프트 (초안)

## 공통 스타일 블록 — 모든 프롬프트 앞에 붙인다

```
Flat vector travel illustration in the style of a modern silkscreen /
risograph print. Limited palette of 4-6 flat colors, no gradients except
one soft sky wash. Visible fine paper grain and subtle screenprint
texture over the whole image. Layered silhouettes for depth: sky band,
mid-ground hill or wall, foreground band. High horizon line placed at
about 60-70% of the height. Soft rounded shapes, no outlines except a
single darker accent stroke on the main subject. Calm, warm, editorial
poster mood. Jeju island scenery.
No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark, no people, no faces,
no photorealism, no 3D render, no drop shadows, no busy detail.
Square 1:1 composition, centered subject, generous negative space.
Must stay legible when scaled down to 80x80 pixels.
```

**팔레트 고정값** (브랜드 토큰):
`#2e9b6b` brand green · `#1d6646` deep green · `#f7f2e6` cream ·
카테고리 tint는 각 프롬프트에 명시.

## 1. 음식점 `RESTAURANT` (221건 중 125건이 이미지 없음)

```
<공통 스타일 블록>
Subject: a single ceramic coffee cup on a saucer, seen from a low angle,
with two thin curls of steam rising. The cup is brand green (#2e9b6b)
with a deep green rim (#1d6646). It sits on a warm sand-colored table
band. Sky wash is warm cream (#fbeed6), table band is soft tan (#e3c9a0).
Mood: a quiet island cafe in the afternoon.
```

**작게 쓸 변형**: `Zoom in so the cup fills 55% of the frame; drop the saucer detail.`

## 2. 숙박 `LODGING` (71건 중 62건이 이미지 없음 — 비율이 가장 높다)

```
<공통 스타일 블록>
Subject: one small gabled guesthouse with a deep green roof (#1d6646)
and cream walls (#f7f2e6), a single lit window in brand green, standing
alone on a low grassy hill. Two layered green hill bands behind it
(#2f6d54 front, #26583f back). Pale blue sky (#dbeaf3) with one soft
cream sun disc in the upper right. No fence, no path, no trees.
Mood: a pension on the Jeju coast at golden hour.
```

**작게 쓸 변형**: `Only the house and one hill band; remove the second hill.`

## 3. 문화시설 `CULTURE` (61건 중 58건이 이미지 없음)

```
<공통 스타일 블록>
Subject: the facade of a small museum — a low triangular roof over three
thick columns, rendered as flat silhouette in muted violet-slate
(#6b6191) with a darker roof (#4a4266). Columns are cut out in the
background tint so they read as negative space. Background is pale
lavender (#e7e2f0), foreground plaza band slightly darker (#cfc7de).
Mood: a quiet regional art museum, no visitors.
```

**작게 쓸 변형**: `Three columns only, thicker, roof simplified to one flat triangle.`

## 4. 관광지 `TOURIST_SPOT` (47건 중 36건이 이미지 없음)

```
<공통 스타일 블록>
Subject: a volcanic hill (Jeju oreum) — one main rounded peak with a
smaller peak to its right, in two greens (#3f8f6a front slope,
#2b6b4d shadow slope), with a small pale cream cap on the smaller peak.
Pale blue sky (#cfe6f2) with one soft cream sun disc in the upper left.
Two overlapping foreground grass bands.
Mood: an oreum trail in early morning.
```

**작게 쓸 변형**: `Two peaks only, remove the sun, thicken the slope bands.`
